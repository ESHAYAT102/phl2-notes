# Critical Thinking

Critical thinking means breaking a problem into smaller, testable parts and
choosing a solution based on evidence and constraints.

- **Choose appropriate tools:** Use the simplest tool that meets the goal.
- **Identify edge cases:** Consider unusual inputs and failure modes before implementation.
- **Verify assumptions:** Test the facts that the solution depends on.
